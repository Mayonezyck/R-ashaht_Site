The player journey

From your first table to your first job

The bot records results; you play the mahjong itself in FFXIV. Everything after that is buttons.

  1. Start

    /start registers your character and home world, gives you a free starter soul crystal, and walks you through your first event.

  2. 東

    Host or join a casual four-player table with /game create, or sign up for a tournament.

  3. 5

    Level up

    Every confirmed game pays EXP into one shared character level. Casual games pay 100 EXP, +50 for 1st, +20 for 2nd.

  4. Take up a job

    At level 5, /job switch to any job whose soul crystal you own. Switch back any time.

  5. 🐣

    Spend & collect

    Earn gil, check it privately with /balance, buy in /shop, and summon minions with /minion.

Play

Casual tables and full tournaments

Casual games

No event or admin needed. Any four registered players can fill a table.

  • /game create posts a lobby and seats you as host. Others press Join game, or find it with /game list.
  • With four seated, the host presses Start game. Play the hanchan in FFXIV.
  • Everyone presses Enter my score with their final points. Scores start at 25,000 each and must total 100,000.
  • All four press Confirm scores. Changing any score clears every confirmation. The fourth confirmation saves the result and pays rewards.
  • /game history lists your ten most recent games. One active casual game per player.

Tournaments

Staff run the event; players mostly press buttons.

  • Sign up with /signup. When the event is full you join the waitlist, and the oldest waiting player is promoted if someone withdraws.
  • Check in with the button on the posted roster, or /checkin.
  • Seated rounds. The first round plans seating for the whole event (four rounds by default), cutting repeat opponents and balancing seats and byes. /table my shows your seat.
  • Table threads. Each table gets its own thread with a live score card. All four players enter and confirm before the result counts.
  • Standings and playoffs. /standings totals points; Top 4 or Top 8 can go on to a FINAL or SEMIFINAL playoff event.
  • Prizes. Events can have a prize list (for example final points > 0 → 1 soul crystal). Items and gil land in inventories when the event finishes; /prizes shows the rules.

Admins can correct, void, or restore a result, skip a table, settle a tie at the playoff cut, and replace a player mid-event. Every repair is audited.

Progression

One level, nine jobs, and your own gil

Levels and jobs

You have one character level per server, shared by every job. Everyone starts as an Adventurer. From level 5, /job switch takes up any job whose soul crystal you own, and you can switch between your jobs, or back to Adventurer, whenever you like. Switching never changes your level.

Claim one free crystal with /job starter or during /start. More come from prizes, reward plans, admins, and the shop. /jobs shows your level, EXP bar, crystals, and each job's pixel art.

Gil

Gil is the basic currency, shown with the in-game gil icon. The default casual plan pays +100 gil per game, prize lists can award it, and admins can grant it.

  • /balance shows your gil and tokens. It is always private and has no player option, so nobody else can look up yours.
  • Gil never appears in /inventory, which lists your items such as soul crystals.
  • Store listings and summon pools can be priced in gil or in tokens like the Halloween Token.
Store & minions

Spend it on crystals, summons, and companions

The store

Every server has an ordinary store that is always open. Staff can also run one seasonal store at a time, such as a Halloween store.

  • /shop opens the store privately, anywhere. Staff can also post a storefront in a channel with a buy button per listing.
  • While a seasonal store is open, a switch button opens it privately for you without changing the posted message.
  • Every buy button opens a private confirmation with the price and your balance. Nothing is spent until you press Confirm purchase.
  • Items go straight to your inventory. Summon listings give one random minion from their pool.

Minions

Minions are collectible companions. Staff add each one to the server's roster from a 384×384 PNG and decide which summon pools offer it. Summon with tokens or gil; every remaining copy is equally likely, so nearly sold-out minions get rare.

Llimited LYIminion code #0007serial · shiny

Limited minions (L) have a fixed number of copies and can sell out; unlimited ones (U) never run out. Serials #0001–#0010 are shiny: their art becomes an animated holographic loop with a drifting rainbow tint, a sweeping sheen, a halo, and sparkles.

/minion opens your companion panel:

  • Summon from an open pool, with a confirm step that shows the cost and your balance first.
  • Book shows your collection, with ??? for minions you have not found.
  • View looks up any copy by ID. Pools shows what's left.
  • Change companion picks the minion on your card. Flex posts it publicly in the channel.

For admins

Running it from Discord

Admin-only controls live under /admin; /tournament, /round, and /config are their own admin groups. Staff-only help sections appear in /help for staff.

Events

/tournament create → open signup → start-checkin → start → /round create. Rounds advance manually; finish with /tournament finish or /round playoff.

Repairs

/admin correct-score, void-score, skip-table, tiebreak, and replace-player, each with a reason and an audit trail.

Rewards and prizes

/admin reward add|remove|reset edits the casual and tournament plans. /admin prize add builds an event's prize list.

Store and minions

/admin store post, add-item, add-summon, seasonal-open. /admin roster add with a 384×384 PNG, then /admin pool create|add|start|stop.

Gil and items

/admin gil grant|remove and /admin inventory grant|remove, with a reason; both are audited.

Backups

The database is backed up daily at 04:00. /admin backup now takes one on demand and /admin backup status shows recent ones and the last restore drill. Restores happen on the server, never from Discord.
